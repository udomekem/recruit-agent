# LinkedIn Post Brief

This workspace does not write or publish posts. It prepares the raw material.
The owner writes and publishes the post through their own process.

```markdown
# Phase N Post Brief: <angle from the plan>

## One line hook candidates (3)

## The one number or surprise
Real figure from the eval report or a real failure found.

## What I built (2 to 3 sentences, plain)

## What broke or surprised me
Specific, honest.

## What the evals showed
Table or 2 to 3 figures, with the date and dataset size.

## What it does not do yet
Honest limits.

## Visuals to capture
Screens, trace screenshot, eval chart, short clip. Redact anything personal.

## Try it
How a stranger can test this phase (or "not public yet").
```

Rules: only claim numbers that appear in the eval report. Use synthetic candidates
in every visual.
